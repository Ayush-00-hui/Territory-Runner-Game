⏱️ MINUTE 1 (3:00 – 4:00) : Athletic Ergonomics & Design Philosophy 3:00 - 4:00
"Good morning respected examiners. My name is Gurudutt, and in Territory Runner, I led the UI/UX design, visual design system, tactical map interface, and athletic ergonomics built with Flutter.

When an athlete runs outdoors, they face extreme visual and physical constraints that break typical app designs: harsh direct sunlight washes out washed-out pastel screens, and physical bouncing and sweaty fingers make standard small buttons impossible to tap reliably.

To solve this, I designed our bespoke Athletic Minimal Design System: Ink and Pacer Blue. We rejected generic white backgrounds and colorful arcade clutter, building a calm, high-contrast tactical dark interface inspired by aviation heads-up displays."

⏱️ MINUTE 2 (4:00 – 5:00) : Tactical Map HUD & 1400ms Hold Gesture 4:00 - 5:00
"Our color architecture is built around three calibrated tokens: a deep carbon canvas (#090B0F) which eliminates screen glare and minimizes OLED battery consumption; crisp Pacer Blue (#4C8DFF) for sovereign athlete trails and conquered sectors; and Coral (#FF7A59) for contested rival boundaries.

On the Tactical Map HUD, dynamic vector polylines and polygons update continuously at 60 FPS. I isolated rendering layers using Flutter RepaintBoundary widgets, preventing heavy map tile re-renders on every GPS tick.

To solve the sweaty-finger problem, I eliminated the standard 'Finish' button. In outdoor testing, runners frequently stop runs by mistake. I created a circular 1400-millisecond Hold-to-Finish gesture with intermediate haptic feedback pulses every 350 milliseconds, requiring deliberate physical intent."

GURUDUTT — Examiner Viva Defense Cheat Sheet

Top 4 killer examiner questions with rapid 15-second oral defenses
Examiner Defense
Q1: Why did you build a custom dark theme instead of using standard Material 3 Light Theme?
15-Second Defense: "Athletic usability: white and pastel screens wash out completely in direct noon sunlight and cause severe pupil glare. Furthermore, running GPS apps consume significant battery; our Ink `#090B0F` canvas turns off OLED pixels on modern AMOLED displays, reducing screen power drain by up to 35% during long outdoor runs."
📁 Implemented in: lib/core/theme/app_theme.dart
Q2: How does the 1400ms hold gesture prevent accidental input during exercise?
15-Second Defense: "Biomechanical tremor: runners in motion with sweaty fingers frequently register accidental double-taps or pocket-rubs on standard modal dialogs. Our 1400ms hold gesture requires continuous finger contact, animates an SVG circular progress ring, and pulses haptic ticks every 350ms, confirming deliberate physical intent without stopping their run."
📁 Implemented in: lib/features/gameplay/territory_service.dart
Q3: How do you keep the tactical map rendering at 60 FPS without frame stutter during live GPS updates?
15-Second Defense: "By using isolated Flutter `RepaintBoundary` widgets. The static map tiles, sovereign territory polygons, and live breadcrumb trail are decoupled into separate CustomPainters. When a new GPS waypoint arrives at 1 Hz, Flutter repaints only the dynamic breadcrumb vector layer while reusing cached raster tile GPU textures."
📁 Implemented in: lib/features/map/widgets/map_layers.dart
Q4: How does the UI handle area reconciliation if the server's PostGIS area differs from the local preview?
15-Second Defense: "Via our non-intrusive AppToast notification framework. The local preview displays an instant optimistic polygon. When the HTTP 201 response returns the authoritative PostGIS area, if the variance exceeds 0.5%, an AppToast gently reconciles the metric on screen without interrupting the athlete's workout summary."
📁 Implemented in: lib/ui/widgets/app_toast.dart
