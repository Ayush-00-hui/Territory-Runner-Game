AVINASH — Examiner Viva Defense Cheat Sheet

Top 4 killer examiner questions with rapid 15-second oral defenses
Examiner Defense
Q1: What is the time complexity of the Lasso Loop Closure detection algorithm?
15-Second Defense: "Naive intersection testing is $O(N^2)$, which freezes phones with thousands of waypoints. We optimize it to $O(M)$ by only testing the latest GPS segment against historical segments within our 25-meter bounding box. This reduces candidate checks to fewer than 20 segments, running in under 0.2 milliseconds at 60 FPS."
📁 Implemented in: lib/features/gameplay/polygon_enclosure_engine.dart
Q2: Why does the Planar Shoelace formula work on Earth's curved spherical surface?
15-Second Defense: "Because human athletic runs are localized ($< 5\text{ km}$ across), Earth's curvature error is negligible (<0.2%) when longitude is scaled by the cosine of the mean latitude. Across our 15 golden fixtures, the area difference between client Shoelace and server PostGIS ellipsoidal geodesics is proven to stay strictly below 0.5%."
📁 Implemented in: backend/internal/geo/shoelace.go
Q3: How does the Jordan Curve Theorem work in your Ray-Casting Donut Hole Classifier?
15-Second Defense: "The Jordan Curve Theorem proves any closed planar loop divides space into inside and outside. We cast a semi-infinite horizontal ray from a candidate interior point. If it crosses the polygon boundary an odd number of times, the point is inside; an even count means outside. This allows us to exclude lakes and buildings from territory claims."
📁 Implemented in: lib/features/gameplay/donut_hole_classifier.dart
Q4: How did you structure the software documentation and cross-platform verification fixtures?
15-Second Defense: "I structured the documentation into clear SRS specifications, architectural data flows, and 15 standardized JSON golden test fixtures. Both our Dart and Go test suites ingest these identical JSON files, ensuring that any algorithmic change in Go or Flutter is verified against the same ground truth."
📁 Implemented in: test_fixtures/ & docs/VERIFICATION.md
